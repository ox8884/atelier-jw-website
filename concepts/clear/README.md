## Variant: Clear House

### Design stance
A bright, direct company site that explains Atelier JW and its ventures before asking the visitor to feel a mood.

### Key choices
- Layout: clear split hero, two venture cards, structured company facts
- Typography: large but readable system grotesk
- Color: cool white, charcoal, single blue accent
- Interaction: company snapshot toast

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: clarity, professionalism, immediate understanding
- Weak at: less personal and less visually distinctive

### Best for
- Banks, suppliers, partners, wholesale contacts, and visitors who need to understand the company quickly
