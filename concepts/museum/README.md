## Variant: Cultural Brand House

### Design stance
A reference-led direction inspired by the graphic language of the Frans Hals Museum website without copying its identity.

### Key choices
- Layout: deep-color masthead, poster-like negative space, overlapping image and information card
- Typography: chunky, compressed system sans with bold hierarchy
- Color: deep teal, peach, coral, mint, cream
- Interaction: full-screen menu and short brand-story message

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: distinctiveness, cultural personality, memorable color and layout
- Weak at: intentionally unconventional and less corporate

### Best for
- Atelier JW as a creative family brand house spanning products, food, and hospitality
