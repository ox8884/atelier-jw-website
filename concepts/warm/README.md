## Variant: Warm Neighborhood

### Design stance
A family and food-led direction that makes Jay, Wave, hospitality, and the future bakery feel central.

### Key choices
- Layout: warm split hero, story section, photographic venture chapters
- Typography: friendly, heavy sans-serif with comfortable body copy
- Color: paper, forest green, brick, muted amber
- Interaction: welcome toast

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: warmth, emotional connection, bakery and café storytelling
- Weak at: Forge Kitchen can feel secondary

### Best for
- A future where the bakery or café becomes the public-facing heart of Atelier JW
