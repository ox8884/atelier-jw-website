## Variant: Bold Brand House

### Design stance
A graphic and confident brand-house concept that clearly frames Atelier JW as the parent of multiple ventures.

### Key choices
- Layout: cobalt statement block, now/next image cards, strong venture tiles
- Typography: compressed oversized sans-serif
- Color: light gray, true charcoal, cobalt accent
- Interaction: brand principle pulse

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: distinctiveness, memorable identity, brand architecture
- Weak at: less intimate and more design-forward

### Best for
- A modern parent company that wants Forge Kitchen and future ventures to feel equally intentional
